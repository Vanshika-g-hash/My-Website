Understanding climate change

The basic structure of the climate problem

01 • Atmosphere

Greenhouse effect

Greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide and methane absorb outgoing heat and influence Earth's energy balance.

02 • Oceans

Heat & acidity

Oceans absorb large amounts of excess heat and carbon dioxide, affecting sea levels and marine ecosystems.

03 • People

Health & livelihoods

Climate hazards can affect health, infrastructure, agriculture, water security and economic activity.

Climate drivers

Major sources of greenhouse gas emissions

01 • Energy

Power & heat

Burning coal, oil and gas for electricity and heat releases greenhouse gases.

Why it matters

Cleaner electricity and lower energy demand can reduce emissions across many sectors.

02 • Industry

Materials & production

Steel, cement, chemicals and manufacturing require large amounts of energy.

Why it matters

Efficient processes, cleaner electricity, material reuse and lower-carbon technologies can reduce industrial emissions.

03 • Transport

Movement of people

Cars, trucks, ships and aircraft depend heavily on fossil fuels.

Why it matters

Public transport, walking, cycling, efficient vehicles and electrification can reduce emissions.

04 • Land & food

Agriculture & land use

Deforestation, livestock, fertilizers and land-use change affect climate.

Why it matters

Protecting forests, improving soil management and reducing food waste can reduce environmental pressure.

05 • Buildings

Homes & workplaces

Heating, cooling, lighting and appliances create significant energy demand.

Why it matters

Insulation, efficient equipment, smart design and clean electricity can reduce energy demand.

06 • Waste

Consumption & waste

Producing, transporting and disposing of products uses resources and energy.

Why it matters

Reducing consumption, repairing, reusing and recycling can reduce resource and energy pressure.

Solutions & action

Practical areas where climate action can happen

01

Clean energy

Expand renewable and other low-carbon electricity while improving energy efficiency and modernizing power systems.

02

Better transport

Improve public transport, walking and cycling while supporting efficient and electric vehicles.

03

Protect nature

Protect forests, wetlands, soils and biodiversity while restoring damaged ecosystems.

04

Efficient buildings

Improve insulation, cooling, heating, lighting and building design to reduce energy demand.

05

Cleaner industry

Improve material efficiency, reuse resources and develop lower-carbon industrial processes.

06

Climate resilience

Prepare communities for heat, flooding, water stress and other climate-related hazards.

What can we do?

Climate action happens at many levels. Individual choices are useful, while businesses, cities and governments can create larger system-wide changes.

Use energy wisely
Improve efficiency and reduce unnecessary energy use.
Choose cleaner mobility
Walk, cycle, use public transport or choose efficient vehicles.
Protect nature
Protect forests, ecosystems and biodiversity.
Reduce waste
Repair, reuse and recycle products and materials.
Support climate solutions
Encourage responsible businesses and climate-friendly policies.
Prepare communities
Strengthen resilience to heat, floods and water stress.
